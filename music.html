FREEVXRSE

Illustrated in stark, graphic-novel black and white, FREEVXRSE places DussT at a speaker’s podium above a shadowy congregation holding forth their hearts, brains, and lungs in profound sacrifice. The track embodies the pain of feeling commodified by modern culture, calling instead for radical creative freedom.
